# 🎓 AI Teaching Features - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 What's New for Teachers

### 1. 📚 Quản lý Giáo trình (Lecture Management)
**Path**: `/teacher/lecture-upload`

**What You Can Do:**
- 📤 Upload course materials (PDF, videos, presentations, documents)
- ✍️ Add metadata (title, description, topics, duration)
- 🤖 AI automatically analyzes your content
- 📋 See AI-generated summaries and key points
- 🗑️ Delete or manage your lectures

**Benefits:**
- Save time on content organization
- AI extracts key topics automatically
- Prepare content for question generation

### 2. 🤖 Tạo Quiz từ AI (AI Quiz Generator)
**Path**: `/teacher/ai-quiz-generator`

**What You Can Do:**
- ❓ Generate questions from lectures or topics
- 🎯 Control difficulty level (easy, medium, hard)
- ✏️ Edit questions before approving
- 👍 Approve or reject AI-generated questions
- 📦 Create quizzes from approved questions
- 📊 Track question usage statistics

**Workflow:**
```
1. Click "Generate Questions"
2. Select lecture or enter topic
3. Choose quantity and difficulty
4. Review generated questions
5. Approve/reject/edit as needed
6. Select questions for quiz
7. Click "Create Quiz"
8. Publish to students
```

**Benefits:**
- Save hours creating questions manually
- Ensure question quality with review process
- Consistent question formatting
- Track which questions work best

### 3. 📊 Thống kê & Insights (Analytics Dashboard)
**Path**: `/teacher/ai-analytics`

**What You Can See:**
- 👥 **Class Statistics**
  - Total number of students
  - Average grade
  - Pass rate percentage
  - Attendance rate

- 📈 **Grade Distribution**
  - Pie chart showing grade ranges
  - Excellent | Good | Average | Poor

- ⚠️ **At-Risk Students**
  - Students likely to fail
  - Risk levels: High | Medium | Low
  - Current grades and reasons
  - Attendance rates

- 📚 **Topics Analysis**
  - Weak topics (students struggle with)
  - Strong topics (students excel at)
  - Difficulty levels
  - Student count per topic

- 💡 **AI Recommendations**
  - Actionable insights based on data
  - Suggestions for intervention
  - Teaching strategies

**Benefits:**
- Identify struggling students early
- Focus on weak areas in class
- Make data-driven decisions
- Personalize teaching approach

## 🔄 Example Workflows

### Workflow 1: Create Quiz from Uploaded Lecture (30 minutes)
```
1. Go to "Quản lý Giáo trình"
   ↓
2. Upload your lecture (PDF, video script, etc.)
   ↓
3. Add title, description, topics, duration
   ↓
4. Click "Upload" - Wait for AI analysis
   ↓
5. See "Analysis Status" change to "Completed"
   ↓
6. Go to "Tạo Quiz từ AI"
   ↓
7. Click "Generate Questions"
   ↓
8. Select your lecture
   ↓
9. Set difficulty & quantity (e.g., "5 medium questions")
   ↓
10. Review questions one by one
    - Click "Approve" for good questions
    - Click "Reject" for bad questions
    - Click "Edit" to modify
   ↓
11. Select approved questions (checkbox)
    ↓
12. Click "Create Quiz (X)"
    ↓
13. Enter quiz title, description, due date
    ↓
14. Click "Create Quiz"
    ↓
15. Go to Assignments → Publish to students
```

### Workflow 2: Identify At-Risk Students (5 minutes)
```
1. Go to "Thống kê & Insights"
   ↓
2. Check "Class Statistics" card
   ↓
3. Scroll to "⚠️ Students at Risk" section
   ↓
4. View risk levels and reasons:
   - "High Risk" students: Consider personalized support
   - "Medium Risk" students: Monitor closely
   - "Low Risk" students: On track
   ↓
5. Click on "Weak Topics" section
   ↓
6. Plan lessons focusing on weak topics
   ↓
7. Follow AI recommendations
   ↓
8. Schedule extra sessions or tutoring
```

### Workflow 3: Plan Teaching Strategy (10 minutes)
```
1. Go to "Thống kê & Insights"
   ↓
2. Review all statistics and charts
   ↓
3. Identify weak topics where majority struggles
   ↓
4. Check strong topics for enrichment
   ↓
5. Read AI recommendations
   ↓
6. Take action:
   - Schedule remedial sessions for weak topics
   - Create additional practice materials
   - Pair strong students with struggling ones
   - Adjust teaching pace
   ↓
7. Upload new lecture materials
   ↓
8. Generate questions for mid-term assessment
```

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Lecture Upload
- ✅ Use descriptive titles and topics
- ✅ Include lecture transcripts for text-based content
- ✅ Set appropriate difficulty levels
- ✅ Regular uploads help AI understand your course
- ❌ Avoid very short or unclear content

### For Question Generation
- ✅ Generate more questions than you need, then select best
- ✅ Review and edit questions for context relevance
- ✅ Use mix of difficulties for comprehensive quizzes
- ✅ Approve quality questions for future reuse
- ✅ Track which questions get high accuracy rates

### For Analytics
- ✅ Check dashboard weekly (not weekly)
- ✅ Focus interventions on high-risk students
- ✅ Use recommendations for curriculum planning
- ✅ Compare class patterns over time
- ✅ Combine with student attendance data

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How long does AI analysis take?**
A: Usually 1-5 minutes depending on file size

**Q: Can I edit AI-generated questions?**
A: Yes! Click the question to edit any part

**Q: How often should I check analytics?**
A: Weekly checks are recommended for best insights

**Q: Can I regenerate questions?**
A: Yes, generate new ones anytime using same lecture

**Q: What if I don't like the suggestions?**
A: You control everything - approve or reject any content

**Q: How does AI know question difficulty?**
A: Analyzes content, student performance, and Bloom's taxonomy

## 📞 Need Help?

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Problem**: "Failed to generate questions"
- Solution: Make sure your lecture has text/content

**Problem**: "No at-risk students showing"
- Solution: Generate analytics first (click "Generate Insights")

**Problem**: "Can't upload large file"
- Solution: Split file into smaller chunks or use text input

**Problem**: "Analytics showing old data"
- Solution: Click "Generate Insights" to refresh data
